## Why this project feels different

Arc/frame is not a flat brochure site. It is designed like a studio pitch in motion:

- big statements and strong visual rhythm
- featured work that feels curated, not dumped into a grid
- pricing and services that stay clear without feeling stiff
- contact and CTA sections that feel human
- extra pages that add depth: CV, sales roadmap, and Figma guide

The goal is simple: make the brand feel memorable after one scroll.

## Design direction

The site leans into:

- editorial-style headings
- animated reveals and motion-led sections
- premium but approachable copy
- gradients, mesh layers, contrast, and atmospheric surfaces
- mobile-friendly layouts with a custom visual identity

If the brand brief was "make it feel alive," this project is the answer.

```text
Loud enough to stand out.
Clean enough to feel premium.
Structured enough to convert.
```

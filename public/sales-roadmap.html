01
The Blueprint

DECONSTRUCTION

Break the skill → find the leverage point
Core micro-skills of sales
Skill 01 · Critical

Problem-First Framing

Leading with their pain, not your product. Every word you say must be about them. This is the single highest-leverage habit in sales.

Skill 02 · Critical

Active Listening & Qualification

Asking sharp questions and shutting up. Identifying if the prospect actually has the problem, the budget, and the authority to say yes.

Skill 03 · Critical

Objection Handling

Responding to "not interested / too expensive / I'll think about it" without sounding desperate. Most calls die here.

Skill 04 · Critical

The Close (Ask for the Next Step)

Getting a specific commitment — a meeting, a callback, a yes — rather than leaving on vague terms like "I'll check and call you back."

Skill 05 · Supporting

Tonality & Vocal Pacing

Sounding calm, confident, and unhurried. Not robotic. Not desperate. The way you say it matters as much as what you say.

Skill 06 · Supporting

Follow-Up Discipline

Most deals close on the 5th–8th touchpoint. Knowing when and how to follow up without being annoying.

Minimum effective dose — your 20%
Focus only on these four things right now
  • Opening with a problem statement, not a product pitch ("I noticed your Google listing has no reviews...")
  • Asking one sharp diagnostic question and actually listening to the full answer
  • Having one pre-prepared response to "I'm not interested" that earns 20 more seconds
  • Ending every call with a specific ask: "Can we do a quick 10-minute call Thursday at 5pm?"
Ignore completely for now
  • SPIN Selling methodology
  • Challenger Sale framework
  • Negotiation tactics
  • CRM optimization
  • Email copywriting
  • Sales funnels
  • Closing scripts with 12+ steps
  • Pricing psychology
02
Friction Elimination

SYSTEM SETUP

Make it impossible not to practice
Environment design — 3 moves
Move 01 · Physical

Print the Script, Kill the Screen

Print your Hinglish cold call script on paper. Keep it beside your phone with a pen to mark objections you hear in the field. No tabs, no scrolling during calls.

Move 02 · Digital

One-File Call Log

A single Notes or Notepad file: date, business name, what they said, what you said, outcome. Review it before every session. Friction to track = zero.

Move 03 · Ritual

Fixed Calling Window

Block 10am–12pm daily as untouchable call time. No warm-up browsing before it starts. The constraint removes the "I'll start soon" spiral.

High-volume drills — ugly, fast, effective
1
The Rejection Speedrun

Set a timer for 30 minutes. Goal: collect 10 rejections. Not 10 conversations — 10 clean "no"s. Reframes rejection as the target, not the failure. Kills call reluctance fast.

Daily · Weeks 1–2 · ~30 min
2
The 60-Second Pitch Recording

Record yourself pitching a fake dentist prospect on voice memo. No reading — from memory. Listen back same day. Find the 1 weakest line. Rewrite it. Record again tomorrow.

Daily · Week 1 · 10 min/day
3
Objection Flash Cards

Write each real objection you hear on a paper slip. Shuffle them. Pull one at random and respond out loud — no prep, no pausing. 15 cards in under 10 minutes daily.

Daily · Week 3 · 10 min/day
4
The Close-Only Drill

After any call that reaches a real conversation (even a soft no), immediately replay the last 30 seconds in your head: Did you ask for something specific? If no — write what you should have said. Out loud.

Post-call · All weeks · 2 min each
5
Reframe 3 No's Per Day

Pick 3 calls that ended with rejection. Write a one-line alternate opening that might have performed differently. No rumination — just one line, then move on. Builds pattern recognition without killing morale.

End of day · Weeks 2–4 · 5 min
04
After Hour 20

DELIBERATE PRACTICE

Push past comfort. Close the feedback loop.
Outside-the-comfort-zone exercises
Exercise 01 · Difficulty: Hard

The Upfront Contract Call

At the start of each call, say: "I'm going to ask you two quick questions. If what I offer isn't relevant, I'll tell you honestly and we'll both save time — fair enough?" Forces you to control frame from second one.

Exercise 02 · Difficulty: Hard

Reverse the Objection

When they say "I'm busy", respond with: "That's exactly why I called — busy usually means your front desk is fielding calls that your website should be handling." Requires deep internalization of their problem.

Exercise 03 · Difficulty: Medium

The Price Conversation First

In one call per day, bring up cost before they ask. "Before I tell you what we do — budgets matter, so I want to be upfront." Desensitizes you to the money conversation and filters non-buyers fast.

Exercise 04 · Difficulty: Hard

Call a Warm No (Week 2 Recontact)

Call back someone who rejected you 2 weeks ago with a completely different opening — reference a specific local event or change ("I noticed your competitor on MG Road just launched a new site..."). Tests adaptability.

Fast feedback loops — prevent bad habits
Loop 01 · Daily

The 3-Question Post-Call Review

  • Did I lead with their problem or mine?
  • Did I ask at least one question and actually wait for the full answer?
  • Did I ask for a specific next step at the end?
Loop 02 · Weekly

The Call Log Pattern Audit

  • Which objection killed the most calls this week?
  • At what point in the call did most people disengage?
  • What's the one line I said that kept someone on the longest?
Loop 03 · Bi-Weekly

Record a Real Call (With Permission)

  • Use a basic call recorder app (ACR on Android)
  • Listen back once — note the moment energy dropped
  • Rewrite that section of the script before next session
Loop 04 · Monthly

External Reality Check

  • Share anonymized call notes with one trusted person
  • Ask: "Where would you have hung up?" — not "Was I good?"
  • Post one cold call breakdown in a sales community for critique
The honest metric to track
Track this one number, nothing else

Conversation Rate — the % of calls where the prospect speaks for more than 30 seconds.

Not conversion rate. Not close rate. Conversation rate.

If you can get 1 in 5 people to actually talk, you're in the top 20% of cold callers. Everything else — objections, closes, conversions — is downstream of this. When this number goes up, you're improving. When it stays flat despite volume, something in your opening is broken. Fix the opening first.
